# School Meals Contract

The council renewed a £12m school meals contract for a further five years
without carrying out the mid-term performance review it committed to when the
contract was first awarded.

Committee minutes record the review as "carried forward" at four consecutive
meetings before the renewal item appeared. No review document exists.

Filed under [[Metro]]. The renewal was approved under delegated authority, which
is why it never reached a full council vote and why the minutes are the only
public trace of it.
