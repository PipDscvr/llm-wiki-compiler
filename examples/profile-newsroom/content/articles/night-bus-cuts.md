# Night Bus Cuts

A draft timetable circulated to operators removes 31 of 94 night bus stops, with
the heaviest reductions on the two routes serving the hospital and the depot
district.

Still a draft. The operator consultation closes at the end of the month and the
stop list is expected to change; nobody but the reporter has taken
responsibility for this text yet.

Intended filing is [[Metro]]. Reported by [[Priya Okafor]].
