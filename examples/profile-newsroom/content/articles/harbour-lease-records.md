# Harbour Lease Records

Three commercial berths were leased at between 60% and 70% of the harbour
board's own internal valuation, in a single round of agreements signed within
eleven days of each other.

The valuations are contemporaneous: each lease file contains the board's own
figure alongside the agreed rent. The board has not answered whether the
discount was authorised or by whom.
