# Transit Levy Audit

The transport authority collected £4.1m more in levy receipts over three years
than it spent on the services the levy was raised to fund, according to its own
year-end reconciliations.

The gap does not appear in the authority's published summaries, which report
levy income and transport expenditure on different pages and on different
year-ends. Reconciling them required the underlying ledgers, obtained after a
four-month disclosure request.

coverage respectively.
