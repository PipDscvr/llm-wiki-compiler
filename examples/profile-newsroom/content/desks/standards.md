# Standards

The corrections and sourcing desk, archived in 2025 when its remit was folded
into the duty editor rota.

Archived is a terminal state: the desk takes no new filings. The stories already
filed under it keep their filing, which is the point — archiving a desk records
that it stopped taking work, not that its back catalogue stopped existing. Any
correction to a story filed here now routes through the duty editor, but the
record of who originally answered for it is unchanged.
