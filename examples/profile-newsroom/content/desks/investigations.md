# Investigations

Long-lead records work: procurement, leases, audits, and anything that depends
on a disclosure request rather than an interview. Investigations carries fewer
stories than [[Metro]] and carries them for longer.

The desk exists as its own filing target because ownership outlives publication.
When a leaked document turns out to be genuine six months after the fact, the
question "whose story was this" has to have an answer that does not depend on
which reporter is still on staff.
