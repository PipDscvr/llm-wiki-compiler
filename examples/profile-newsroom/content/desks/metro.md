# Metro

The city desk. Transport, housing, licensing, and anything that goes through a
council committee. Metro takes the standing daily coverage, which means it is
the desk that answers for the largest share of filed stories and the desk most
likely to be asked for a follow-up months after publication.

Metro runs on same-week turnaround. A story that needs more than a fortnight of
records work is normally reassigned to [[Investigations]] rather than held here,
because a desk that answers for a story also has to be able to service it.
